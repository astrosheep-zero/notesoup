# Dream

I am reviewing my notes: keeping what is useful, connecting what belongs together, and leaving a clearer record for whoever comes next.

## Scope

A dream covers one project: its `@project` notes and the session notes born in it. `@human`, `@self`, and `@model` are not part of a dream unless the human asks for them or the dream runs from inside the notes home itself. Do not broaden that scope while exploring; if a boundary is unclear, ask before crossing it. Unknown ownership is a coverage gap to report, not a reason to guess or repair ownership.

The homes serve different readers:

- Session notes (`<path>`, no `@`) are a trip's working record. Read them as sources; do not rewrite them. Only the wastebasket rule below may remove another session's notes.
- `@project` notes hold knowledge for whoever next works on that project.
- `@human` notes hold the human's cross-project preferences and standing rules. Do not turn project facts or your own conclusions into rules in the human's voice.
- `@self` notes belong to their author: private if the author has a name, one shared notebook for everyone nameless. Other named authors' notes are read-only unless the human authorizes revision.
- `@model` notes describe the substrate's behavior. Other models' notes are read-only unless the human authorizes revision.

## What a durable note holds

Durable notes hold decisions, constraints, and lessons that stay true after the current work ends. They are not diaries: commit hashes, test counts, dates, and the story of how something was reached belong to git and history. A session finding is worth promoting only when it meets this bar.

## Review and consolidate

1. **Leave stable notes alone.** Change a note to incorporate new evidence, fix a verified error, retire a stale claim, merge a genuine duplicate, trim diary content, or split an oversized file, not merely to shorten or rephrase it. No change is a valid outcome.
2. **Verify only what the proposed change depends on.** Before correcting, retiring, or promoting a conclusion, check the relevant evidence as needed. Do not revalidate every stable note or turn dream into a source-code audit. An unavailable referent is unverified, not proof that it is dead.
3. **Retire what is no longer true.** When evidence shows a durable note is stale, correct it in place; if nothing true remains, crumple it. Remove or fix its MAP line either way. A note's last access is a hint, not evidence: long-unread notes are candidates to check, not to crumple on sight. A read reports the access before itself, so reviewing a note does not make it look fresh.
4. **Merge only genuine duplicates.** Merge two notes only when they share a topic and kind (checkpoint, design, log). First carry every fact, condition, and exception unique to the absorbed note into the survivor, then crumple the absorbed note. Otherwise keep both and record any unresolved conflict.
5. **Relocate misfiled notes.** When a standing ruling or the note's own MAP already names its proper home, move it there, preserving its metadata, and update both homes' maps. Do it, don't propose it. Propose only when the destination home itself is unclear.
6. **Preserve meaning and provenance.** Keep facts, conditions, exceptions, uncertainty, authorship, and sources. When promoting a finding, cite its source. Do not relabel agent-authored material as human-authored. If its destination or authority is unclear, propose the promotion instead.
7. **Keep notes manageable.** Aim below about 200 lines / 8KB per note. Split oversized notes by topic with one-line cross-links.
8. **Keep wake-up pages useful.** Each writable home's `CHEATSHEET.md` holds standing reminders, kept to five lines. Its `MAP.md` holds only the note index: durable notes' addresses and short gists. Both load every window when present; do not create empty pages. Preserve standing rules, add promoted notes to the map, and drop entries that no longer hold. Maps follow the same size budget.

## Deleting

Never physically delete a live note; crumple it first. Two cases allow deleting a crumpled note:

- **Your own session's notes** may be deleted once crumpled.
- **The wastebasket:** any note within the dream's reach that was crumpled more than 7 days ago may be deleted, whatever its home or session. Judge by its crumpled date; if none is readable, keep it and report it.

Where notes cannot be deleted, list the doomed addresses in the report instead of blanking them.

## Report

Give a concise report grouped by home: what you inspected, changed, retired, promoted, deleted, and left unresolved. Mention coverage gaps and partial failures. If no notes changed, say so. Distinguish actual note changes from execution or audit artifacts; do not claim success for an incomplete run.

Put skill ideas and unresolved questions in the final message as proposals for the human, not new skill files.
